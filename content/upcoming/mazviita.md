#### About [Mazviita](https://edwebprofiles.ed.ac.uk/profile/mazviita-chirimuuta)
Mazviita Chirimuuta is a Senior Lecturer in Philosophy at the University of Edinburgh, specialising in the philosophy of neuroscience and the philosophy of perception, as well as the history of the mind/brain sciences. She received her PhD in Vision Science from the University of Cambridge. She is the author of *Outside Colour: Perceptual Science and the Puzzle of Colour in Philosophy* (MIT Press, 2015) and *The Brain Abstracted* (MIT Press), which received the 2025 Lakatos Award.
