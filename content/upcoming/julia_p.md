#### About [Julia](https://phil.washington.edu/people/julia-pelger)
Julia Pelger is a doctoral student in the Department of Philosophy at the University of Washington. She works in applied ethics and bioethics, with further interests in metaethics, political philosophy, gender studies, climate change and sustainability, and eighteenth-century philosophy, Kant in particular. She holds an M.A. in Philosophy from the University of Washington (2024), and an M.Ed. (2021) and B.A. (2018) from the Rheinische Friedrich-Wilhelms-Universität Bonn.
