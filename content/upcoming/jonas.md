#### About [Jonas](https://www.fernuni-hagen.de/digitalrecht/team/jonas.botta.shtml)
Jonas Botta is Junior Professor of Public Law with a focus on the law of digitalisation and artificial intelligence at FernUniversität in Hagen. His research concerns the legal treatment of new technologies, especially artificial intelligence, the digitalisation of public administration, and the democratic resilience of the constitutional state and its institutions. He studied law at Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin and received his doctorate in 2019 from the German University of Administrative Sciences Speyer with a thesis on data protection on e-learning platforms. Before moving to Hagen he was a research officer at the German Research Institute for Public Administration in Speyer.
