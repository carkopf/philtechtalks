#### About [Renate](https://www.jura.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/ipr/team/Renate_Schaub.html.en)
Renate Schaub holds the Chair of Civil Law, Private International Law and Comparative Law, Commercial and Economic Law at Ruhr-Universität Bochum, where she has been a professor since 2008. Her main areas of research are private law, private international law, comparative law with a focus on Anglo-American law, and commercial law, especially the law of unfair competition and intellectual property. She received her doctorate in 1999 and her habilitation in 2004 from the University of Tübingen, holds an LL.M. from the University of Bristol, and was a professor at the University of Göttingen from 2005 to 2008.
