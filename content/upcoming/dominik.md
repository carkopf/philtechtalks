#### About [Dominik](https://www.philosophie.fb05.uni-mainz.de/junior-prof-dr-dominik-balg/)
Dominik Balg is Junior Professor of Didactics of Philosophy at Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz, where he heads the corresponding working group in the Department of Philosophy. His research concerns moral education, the formation of philosophical judgment, and sceptical and relativist challenges to philosophical education. He is the author of *Wissen, was gut ist: Moralische Bildung im Spannungsfeld zwischen Manipulation und Beliebigkeit* (Metzler, 2024) and, with Leonard Dung, of "Learning alone: language models, overreliance, and the goals of education" (*AI and Ethics*, 2026). He studied philosophy and German at the University of Cologne and Northwestern University, received his doctorate from Cologne in 2018, taught at a Gymnasium, and was Akademischer Rat at the University of Tübingen from 2021 to 2023.
