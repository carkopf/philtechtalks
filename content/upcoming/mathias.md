#### About [Mathias](https://www.hks.harvard.edu/faculty/mathias-risse)
Mathias Risse is Berthold Beitz Professor in Human Rights, Global Affairs and Philosophy at Harvard Kennedy School, where he directs the Carr-Ryan Center for Human Rights. He also directs the Program on the Ethics of Emerging Technologies at the Edmond J. Safra Center for Ethics. His research covers political philosophy, especially global justice, and the philosophy of technology, along with ethics, decision theory, and nineteenth-century German philosophy. His books include *On Global Justice* (2012) and *Political Theory of the Digital Age: Where Artificial Intelligence Might Take Us* (2023). He studied in Bielefeld, Pittsburgh, and Jerusalem, received his PhD from Princeton in 2000, and taught at Yale before joining Harvard in 2002.
